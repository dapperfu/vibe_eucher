# Game Rules

This document explains the complete rules of Euchre, a classic trick-taking card game for 4 players.

## Game Overview

Euchre is a fast-paced card game played with a 24-card deck. The goal is to be the first team to score 10 points by winning tricks and successfully calling trump.

## Players and Teams

- **4 Players**: Arranged around a table
- **2 Teams**: Players sitting across from each other are partners
  - **Team 1**: Players 1 & 3 (sitting across from each other)
  - **Team 2**: Players 2 & 4 (sitting across from each other)

## The Deck

### Cards Used
- **Suits**: Hearts ♥, Diamonds ♦, Clubs ♣, Spades ♠
- **Ranks**: 9, 10, J (Jack), Q (Queen), K (King), A (Ace)
- **Total**: 24 cards (6 cards × 4 suits)

### Card Rankings
**Normal Suits** (when not trump):
- Highest: A (Ace)
- K (King)
- Q (Queen)
- J (Jack)
- 10
- Lowest: 9

**Trump Suit** (when called):
- Highest: Right Bower (Jack of trump suit)
- Left Bower (Jack of same color as trump)
- A (Ace)
- K (King)
- Q (Queen)
- 10
- Lowest: 9

**Non-Trump Suits**:
- All cards rank normally (A > K > Q > J > 10 > 9)

## Game Setup

### Dealing
1. **Dealer**: One player is designated as dealer (rotates each round)
2. **Deal**: 5 cards to each player (20 cards total)
3. **Top Card**: 4 cards remain in deck, top card is revealed

### Trump Selection
1. **First Round**: Players can "order up" the top card to make its suit trump
2. **Second Round**: If no one orders up, players can call any suit as trump
3. **Dealer's Choice**: If no one calls, dealer must pick a trump suit

## Gameplay

### Trump Selection Phase

#### First Round - Ordering Up
- Starting with player to dealer's left, each player can:
  - **Order Up**: Accept the top card as trump (you get the card)
  - **Pass**: Decline to make the top card trump

#### Second Round - Calling Trump
- If no one orders up, starting with player to dealer's left:
  - **Call Trump**: Choose any suit as trump
  - **Pass**: Decline to call trump

#### Dealer's Choice
- If no one calls trump, dealer must pick a suit

### Trick Playing

#### Trick Structure
- **5 Tricks per Round**: Each round consists of 5 tricks
- **Lead**: First player plays any card
- **Follow Suit**: Other players must play cards of the same suit if possible
- **Trump**: If you can't follow suit, you can play any card (including trump)

#### Playing a Trick
1. **Lead**: First player plays any card
2. **Follow Suit**: Each player must play a card of the same suit if they have one
3. **Trump**: If you can't follow suit, you can play any card
4. **Winner**: Highest trump card wins, or highest card of the lead suit if no trump

#### Trick Winner
- **Trump Cards**: Beat all non-trump cards
- **Same Suit**: Higher rank wins
- **Lead Suit**: Wins if no trump was played

### Round Completion
After 5 tricks:
1. **Count Tricks**: Each team counts tricks won
2. **Score Round**: Points awarded based on results
3. **New Round**: Dealer rotates, new cards dealt

## Scoring

### Basic Scoring
- **Win 3-4 Tricks**: 1 point
- **Win All 5 Tricks**: 2 points

### Euchre Bonus
- **Getting Set**: If trump-calling team wins fewer than 3 tricks
- **Bonus Points**: Opposing team gets 2 points

### Game End
- **First to 10**: Team that reaches 10+ points wins
- **Sudden Death**: Game can end mid-round if a team reaches 10

## Strategy

### Trump Selection
- **Strong Hand**: Call trump if you have multiple high cards
- **Weak Hand**: Pass and let others call trump
- **Risk Assessment**: Consider if you can win 3+ tricks

### Card Play
- **Follow Suit**: Always play the same suit when possible
- **Lead High**: Play high cards when leading
- **Save Trump**: Don't waste trump cards unnecessarily
- **Partner Coordination**: Work with your teammate

### Team Play
- **Communication**: Pay attention to partner's plays
- **Trick Counting**: Keep track of team trick totals
- **Risk Management**: Don't risk losing if you're ahead

## Example Game

### Round 1
```
Dealer: Alice
Top Card: Queen of Hearts

Trump Selection:
- Bob: Pass
- Charlie: Pass  
- David: Pass
- Alice: Orders up Hearts (gets Queen of Hearts)

Trump Suit: Hearts
```

### Trick 1
```
Lead: Bob plays 9 of Spades
Charlie: 10 of Spades (follows suit)
David: King of Spades (follows suit)
Alice: Ace of Spades (follows suit)

Winner: Alice (Ace of Spades)
```

### Scoring
```
Team 1 (Alice & Charlie): 3 tricks = 1 point
Team 2 (Bob & David): 2 tricks = 0 points

Game Score: Team 1: 1, Team 2: 0
```

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Strong Trump Hand
**Your Hand**: ['Ace of Diamonds', 'King of Diamonds', 'Jack of Hearts', 'Ten of Spades', 'Nine of Clubs']
**Top Card**: Queen of Diamonds
**Decision**: Order up Diamonds (you have 3 trump cards)

### Scenario 2: Weak Hand
**Your Hand**: ['Nine of Spades', 'Ten of Clubs', 'Queen of Hearts', 'King of Clubs', 'Ace of Hearts']
**Top Card**: Jack of Diamonds
**Decision**: Pass (your hand is weak)

### Scenario 3: Following Suit
**Lead Suit**: Spades
**Your Hand**: ['Ace of Spades', 'Ten of Diamonds', 'Queen of Clubs', 'King of Hearts', 'Nine of Clubs']
**Decision**: Play Ace of Spades (you must follow suit and it's your highest Spade)

## Rules Summary

### Must Do
- Follow suit when possible
- Play exactly one card per trick
- Respect trump selection rules

### Cannot Do
- Play out of turn
- Play invalid cards
- Change trump after selection

### Penalties
- **Reneging**: Playing wrong suit when you could follow (automatic loss of trick)
- **Invalid Trump Call**: Breaking trump selection rules

## Variations

### Stick the Dealer
- If no one calls trump, dealer must pick
- Adds pressure to make trump calls

### Going Alone
- Some variants allow calling "alone" (partner sits out)
- Bonus points for winning alone

### Jokers
- Some variants include jokers as highest trump
- Not used in standard euchre

## Tips for Beginners

1. **Start Simple**: Focus on following suit and basic trump play
2. **Watch Others**: Learn from experienced players
3. **Practice Trump Selection**: This is often the most critical decision
4. **Count Tricks**: Keep track of how many each team has won
5. **Partner Communication**: Work with your teammate

## Advanced Strategies

### Trump Management
- Save trump for critical moments
- Use low trump to win when you can't follow suit
- Coordinate trump usage with partner

### Lead Strategy
- Lead your strongest suit
- Consider partner's likely holdings
- Lead trump to draw out opponent's trump

### Defensive Play
- Protect your partner's tricks
- Don't waste high cards unnecessarily
- Block opponents from winning multiple tricks
